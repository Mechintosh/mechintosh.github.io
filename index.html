Choose your character.

So, this is the pre-made characters I have picked and made for you. There are several classes and races with different levels of dfficulty. As seen in the images, some highlights and drawbacks are shown as well as the painted mini I already had that somewhat fit the character.

Now on to picking. Use the chat to talk amongst yourselves on what you want to do. My suggestion is to have a mix of magic and not as well as a tank and a healer. You can go hard-mode and only play damage dealers with no magic nor healing. I salute you if you do.

Now on to the base idea. This is a one-shot adventure. It is supposed to run for 3-4 hours and then end. I have no intention to keep this going so no need for elaborate backstories as I won't have the time to incorporate them into the story. If you want to make something, feel free. If you want to share, please do.

The story is supposed to be fairly straight forward with a good intro to many mechanics. It is however NOT an elaborate story with hidden agendas and secrets all over the place. With this in mind, and time I might have to rail-road a bit. I might force the story forward if you are not making fast enough progress.

I won't tell you more about the story. Just that there will be times when talking is good, and there will be times when fighting is good. So pick characters with that in mind.

If you have questions about a mechanic or a character, I will be happy to answer them. Now go forth and chose your characters!
